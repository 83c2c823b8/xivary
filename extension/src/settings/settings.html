Support Xivary

I want to keep Xivary minimal and free of unnecessary UI. Even adding a donation link feels fundamentally at odds with that philosophy.

Ideally, I'd like to manage your Library, Following, and Collections through a server so that everything stays synchronized across devices. However, running and maintaining that infrastructure costs money, and I currently don't have the budget to support it.

If you find Xivary useful and would like to help make cross-device synchronization possible in the future, you can support the project through Buy Me a Coffee.

Support is entirely optional. Xivary will remain usable without it.
